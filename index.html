How to play

  • Get your white shape into the green-rimmed hole of the same shape: a circle first, then a square, a triangle, a diamond and stars with more points every level.
  • Aim: press anywhere on the board, pull back the way you'd pull a slingshot and release. The arrow shows direction and power. You can shoot once the puck has stopped (it glows).
  • Scoring: 5,000 points on the 1st shot, 4,000 on the 2nd, down to 1,000 on the 5th. ★★★ = hole in one, ★★ = two or three shots.
  • Pucks (lives): you start with 5 and get a bonus puck on reaching every 5th level. Falling into a dark hole costs one; the purple one moves.
  • Moving pucks: every hit costs 500 points, and every 10th hit in a level costs a puck.
  • Game over: you go back to your last 🚩 checkpoint (levels 1, 6, 11, …) with the score you had there.
  • Practice: tap any level you have reached to replay it without risking your run. Stars count; points and pucks don't.
  • A puck that crosses a hole very fast skims over it, like a putt that lips out.
  • 🎯 Aim guide (G): a dotted path with cushion bounces. Using it caps the level at ★★.
  • ⛳ Behind view (🎥 button or V): a golf-style camera behind the puck. Aim is relative to the camera, and a north-up board map shows the whole table (N or the 🗺 icon: off / small / large).

3D viewing modes

  • Parallel 3D: left image for left eye — cardboard viewers or parallel free-viewing.
  • Cross-Eye 3D: cross your eyes until a third image fuses in the middle.
  • Anaglyph: red/cyan glasses.
  • Smart Glasses (HSBS): 16:9 half side-by-side for RayNeo Air, XREAL, Rokid, TCL — switch the glasses to 3D SBS mode. Tilt the phone in your hand to aim and tap to shoot, or use a Bluetooth gamepad.
  • 🥽 VR: appears in the Meta Quest browser; the board sits on a virtual table, the thumbstick aims and the trigger shoots.
  • 3D Depth scales with the viewing distance; lower it if your eyes strain. Focus moves the screen plane.
